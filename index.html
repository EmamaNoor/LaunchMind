Cook Verification

Every home cook submits identity documents and kitchen photos. Our team reviews and approves each profile before they can list meals — so you always know who's cooking for you.

🥗

Meal Listings with Hygiene Badge

Cooks create detailed meal posts with photos, ingredients, and allergens. Each listing shows a visible hygiene badge after passing our guidelines — trust at a glance.

📍

Location-Based Search

Enter your work address to see only meals from cooks within a defined radius. No more waiting — your lunch arrives fresh and on time, every time.

🔒

Ordering & Prepayment

Order with secure payment, and cooks receive instant notifications to confirm availability. No cash, no hassle — just a smooth transaction from kitchen to desk.

⭐

Rating & Review System

Both parties rate each other after every transaction. Low-rated cooks are suspended pending review — ensuring quality and accountability for everyone.
